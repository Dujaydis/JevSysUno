# Sources and confidence levels

Research conducted 2026-09-18 by three parallel agents. **The `typesafe.ai` and
`docs.typesafe.ai` domains were blocked by this environment's network egress policy**, as
were most secondary blogs and Hacker News. Rather than give up or paper over the gap, the
research routed around it via GitHub, which was reachable.

Read every claim in this repo against the tier it came from.

## Tier 1 — first-party source code, read in full (highest confidence)

These were cloned and read directly. Field names and types taken from here are exact.

- `github.com/typesafe-ai/typesafe-sdk-python` — v0.7.0. Its wire models are generated by
  `datamodel-codegen` from `https://api.typesafe.ai/openapi.json`, i.e. from TypeSafe's real
  OpenAPI spec.
- `github.com/typesafe-ai/typesafe-sdk-js` — v0.6.0.
- `github.com/typesafe-ai/skills` — TypeSafe's own official agent skill, containing their
  prose guidance on question design, composition and confidence handling.

**Everything in `02-api-contract.md` comes from this tier**, which is why it can be trusted
for writing code.

## Tier 2 — independent third-party measurement, read in full (high confidence)

- `github.com/themsquared/jev-benchmark` — n=60 hand-labeled, tool-call risk, with

## A note on the "community" around Jev

Search surfaced a cluster of near-identical repos — `awesome-jev-by-typesafe` and variants
under at least seven unrelated accounts, all created the same week with identical boilerplate
descriptions, plus verbatim mirrors of the official JS SDK under personal accounts. This
pattern reads as templated SEO/AI-generated repo spam rather than organic community
adoption. None were read, and none should be treated as a source. If you go looking for
Jev examples yourself, check the provenance of what you find.

## Known gaps

- No Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA, r/MachineLearning) discussion located.
- TypeSafe's privacy policy exists but its actual terms are unread.
- No large-scale independent reproduction of the vendor's benchmarks has surfaced yet.
- Rate limits and pricing could not be confirmed first-hand at all.
